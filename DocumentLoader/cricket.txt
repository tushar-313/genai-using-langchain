Upon the field of emerald green,
Where dreams are chased and hopes are seen,
The bat swings high, the ball takes flight,
As hearts beat fast beneath the light.

A bowler runs with focused grace,
A batter stands and holds their place,
One perfect shot, a roaring cheer,
A moment fans will hold so dear.

Through wins and losses, joy and pain,
The love of cricket will remain.
For more than just a game we play,
It brings us all together, day by day.

So raise the bat and chase the sun,
Respect the game and have some fun.
For every match, both great and small,
Cricket is a game that unites us all.
